## Overview
*Title:* The Great Gatsby  
*Author:* F. Scott Fitzgerald  
*Published:* 1925  
*Genre:* Tragedy, Realism, Modernism, Social Commentary  

---

## Plot Summary
The Great Gatsby is set in the summer of 1922 and tells the story of *Jay Gatsby*, a mysterious millionaire who throws extravagant parties in hopes of reuniting with his lost love, *Daisy Buchanan*.  
The story is narrated by *Nick Carraway*, a Yale graduate and World War I veteran who rents a house next door to Gatsby on Long Island.  

As Nick becomes drawn into Gatsby’s world of wealth, romance, and deception, he discovers the emptiness that lies beneath the glittering surface of the American Dream.

---

## Main Characters
- *Jay Gatsby* – A wealthy and mysterious man obsessed with winning back Daisy.  
- *Daisy Buchanan* – Gatsby’s former lover, now married to Tom Buchanan.  
- *Nick Carraway* – The novel’s narrator and Gatsby’s neighbor.  
- *Tom Buchanan* – Daisy’s arrogant and unfaithful husband.  
- *Jordan Baker* – Daisy’s friend and a professional golfer.
